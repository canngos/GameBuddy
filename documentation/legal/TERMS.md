continue an open conversation with you.

**We commit to reviewing every report and acting on it within 24 hours of receiving it.**

## 4. Automated screening

Some screening happens before content is published, and it is not perfect:

- **Profile pictures** are checked by an automated classifier before they appear to anyone.
  Images the classifier is confident are sexual are refused. Images it is unsure about are
  held, unpublished, for review by a person.
- **Text** you submit is filtered. Some words are masked; slurs and sexual abuse are
  refused outright. Where text is visible to people you have not matched with — your profile,
  a lobby's name and description — email addresses, links and phone numbers are removed.

Automated screening supplements human review. It does not replace it, and it is not a
guarantee that everything you see has been checked. If something you submitted was refused and
you believe that was wrong, write to us and a person will look at it.
