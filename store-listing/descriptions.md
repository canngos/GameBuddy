## Full description (~1,950/4,000)

```
GameBuddy matches you with gamers who own the same games, play on the same platform, and play the same way — then gets out of the way so you can go play.

Not a server full of strangers. You tell GameBuddy what you play and how, and it shows you real profiles ranked by how well they actually fit. Pass or match. When you both say yes, the conversation opens.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Say what you play — pick your games, your platforms and your play style: tryhard, completionist, chill co-op. Six screens, about a minute.
2. Swipe the deck — real profiles, ranked by how well they fit what you said.
3. Match and play — when you both match, the chat opens. Then go play something.

WHAT MAKES A MATCH A MATCH

• Matched on what you play. GameBuddy learns from the games and play styles on your profile, not from a list of tags you scroll past.
• Platform-aware. PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Switch or mobile — two people with identical libraries on different boxes can't actually play together, so the match takes that into account.
• Lobbies. Open a lobby for the game and time you want to play, or browse open lobbies and join a team that fits.
• Chat that stays private. Message bodies are encrypted at rest — the database stores ciphertext, not your conversation.
• Moderated, and adults only. Every uploaded photo is screened before anyone else sees it. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. You must be 18 or over to hold an account.
• Badges worth having. Missions and badges you earn by actually using the app, shown on your profile. No pay-to-win tiers.
• Free to play, properly. Matching, chat and lobbies are free, with a daily limit on likes. Gold is optional — advanced filters, unlimited likes and a few cosmetic extras. It does not gate the thing you came for.

NOT A DATING APP

GameBuddy matches people on the games they play and how they play them — a squad, a co-op partner, a raid group. It is built around playing together.

GameBuddy is free, moderated, and 18+.
Built in Finland by one person.
```
